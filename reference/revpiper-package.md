# revpiper: Systematic Review Data Processing and Presentation Pipelines

Takes systematic review teams from extracted data to manuscript-ready
tables and figures through user-authored specifications: a data
dictionary describing their extraction files, declared assembly and
automated cleaning, audited manual corrections, and a tiered,
re-runnable derivation layer.
